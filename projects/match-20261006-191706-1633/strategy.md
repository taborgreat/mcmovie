# Edit strategy: Nature documentary (`nature-doc`)

A nature documentary about the match. Observe the players as creatures: where they go, what they gather, how they hunt, how they hide. Never a game term, never a kit name; a stomp is a creature dropping on its prey from a height. Enchanting music to open, the dramatic song for the kill.

Frame: **machinima-story**. Camera style the program will lean to: wide.

## Rules the checker holds this film to

- Narrator voice: `"narrator": { "voice": "stock:storyteller" }`.
- No kit talk at all: never the word "kit" and never a kit's name, in the narration, the title or the logline. People with names, not classes.
- Point of view: write every line as a nature documentary: the players are creatures observed in their habitat, their habits and instincts described gently and precisely; the winner is simply the one best adapted. (The editor's pass judges this; the voice above is only the sound of it.)
- Narration density: between 55 and 95 words a minute of film (all spoken words / film length).
- Raw numbers rarely: at most one narrator line in four.
- Between 2 and 6 segments.
- The first segment's music is enchanting, fairytale, wonder or warm (its `"music"` field).
- The finale song is "hide-seek" (set by the program; its segments are in digest.md).

Everything else follows prompts/match.md. These rules win where they differ from it.
