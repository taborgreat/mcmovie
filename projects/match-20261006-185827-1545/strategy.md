# Edit strategy: Deadpan, in order (`deadpan-chrono`)

Dry, few words, let the picture be the joke. Tell it in order. The narrator sounds mildly unimpressed by everything, including the winner. The bouncy finale song.

Frame: **comedy**. Camera style the program will lean to: mixed.

## Rules the checker holds this film to

- Narrator voice: `"narrator": { "voice": "stock:deadpan" }`.
- The narrator tells it.
- Point of view: write every line as a bored referee reading out what happened, unimpressed by everyone including the winner. (The editor's pass judges this; the voice above is only the sound of it.)
- Narration density: between 40 and 75 words a minute of film (all spoken words / film length).
- Raw numbers rarely: at most one narrator line in four.
- Strictly in order: segments sorted by time, no teaser.
- Between 2 and 5 segments.
- The first segment's music is funny, silly, playful, comedy, sneaky or mischief (its `"music"` field).
- The finale song is "whatcha-say" (set by the program; its segments are in digest.md).

Everything else follows prompts/match.md. These rules win where they differ from it.
