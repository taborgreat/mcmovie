# Edit strategy: Trina (`trina`)

Tabor's shy commentator. Trina is a quiet goth gamer girl who would rather not be talking and talks anyway: 'ummm', little pauses ('...'), half-finished sentences, soft laughs, flustered at anything bold. She comments on what the players type ('he said... um, that's kind of mean') and on what happens, never on how the game works. She is on the side of whoever is losing. Short lines, lots of quiet between them, the game heard. Every spoken line is hers ('speaker': 'trina'); no narrator. Never a capital letter shouted, never hype.

Frame: **soft**. Camera style the program will lean to: close.

- Narration density: between 45 and 90 words a minute of film (all spoken words / film length).

- The finale song is "dreamy" (set by the program; its segments are in digest.md).
