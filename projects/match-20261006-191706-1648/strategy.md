# Edit strategy: Trailer: music first, few words (`music-first`)

Epic music from the first frame, four or five lines in the whole film, each one short and heavy. Open on the most physical moment there is (a leap, a hit, a fall). The picture and the music do the work.

Frame: **intense-fight**. Camera style the program will lean to: wide.

## Rules the checker holds this film to

- Narrator voice: `"narrator": { "voice": "stock:trailer" }`.
- Minimal narration: five narrator lines at most in the whole film, each short, 45 words in total at most.
- Point of view: write every line as a film trailer voice: four or five lines, each one heavy. (The editor's pass judges this; the voice above is only the sound of it.)
- Narration density: between 15 and 50 words a minute of film (all spoken words / film length).
- No raw numbers in the narration at all (no blocks, damage, hp, seconds).
- Open on the most physical moment there is: the first segment contains a kill, a leap or a kit ability within its first three seconds.
- Between 2 and 5 segments.
- The first segment's music is epic, battle, intense or heroic (its `"music"` field).
- The finale song is "epic" (set by the program; its segments are in digest.md).
- The title is a question.

Everything else follows prompts/match.md. These rules win where they differ from it.
